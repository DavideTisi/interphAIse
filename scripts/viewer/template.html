Coverage, provenance and scientific limits

Archived model results, not experimental adhesion. Wadh uses independently relaxed separated slabs; Wsep uses fixed geometries split from the relaxed contact. Neither validates coating transport or polar electrostatics. Energies appear only on their own relaxed contact. Original MP, PET-MAD references and historical pilots retain separate provenance. Both contacts are formally polar; completed checks and remaining limitations are listed above.

Atomic radii are scaled for visibility; bonds are not inferred. Cell edges include vacuum. Repeat and wrap change the display only. XYZ downloads preserve the saved coordinates and verified state-local labels/constraints. A fixed mask on an initial interface is the planned mask recorded by that run. Candidate structures shared across environments are listed once per MP ID and geometry; polymorphs keep separate IDs. This catalogue contains no thermodynamic benchmark outcomes.
