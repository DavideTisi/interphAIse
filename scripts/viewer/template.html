interphAIse / ATOMISTIC MODELS

Structure explorer

Explore bulk candidates and both coating contacts: cathode | coating and electrolyte | coating. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and hover over an atom to inspect its coordinates. Side-by-side views share the same camera and scale.

Geometry inspection, not a validated adhesion result. The pilots have polar surfaces and unrelaxed bulk reference lattices; surface and cell convergence checks remain open. Atomic radii are scaled for visibility; bonds are not inferred. Cell edges represent the saved simulation cell, including vacuum. Replication shows translated copies along a and b; it does not relax atoms. Wrap a/b brings atoms into the periodic in-plane cell for display only. Downloads contain the original selected geometry, not displayed copies. Fixed-atom colouring shows stored FixAtoms constraints; the initial file does not store the later relaxation constraints. Bulk structures come from the frozen Materials Project pool. This viewer contains no benchmark outcome table.
